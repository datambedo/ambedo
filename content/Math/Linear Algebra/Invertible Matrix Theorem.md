Given a $n \times n$ matrix $M$, we have the following by the **invertible matrix theorem**:
$$
M \text{ is invertible} \iff
\begin{cases}
\text{Col}(M) = \mathbb{R}^n \\
\dim(\text{Col}(M)) = n = \text{rank}(M) \\
\text{Null}(M) = \{ \vec{0} \} \\
\dim(\text{Null}(M)) = 0 = \text{nullity}(M)
\end{cases}
$$
