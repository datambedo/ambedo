In classical statistical inference, the data has some assumed distribution with parameters.
These parameters are unknown constants rather than random variables.
Estimators are **functions** of observed data that provide estimates of these parameters.

*Expectation and variance are population concepts! They require full knowledge of the population distribution (PDF)*



## Bias
An estimator $\hat \theta$ is **unbiased*** if:
$$E[\hat \theta]=\theta$$
